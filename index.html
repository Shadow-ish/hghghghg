Price source settings

Cryptocurrency prices (in US dollars, refreshed every 30 seconds) come from CoinGecko and need no key. Keys stay in this browser and are only sent to their own provider. US prices refresh every 15 seconds. Alpha Vantage free keys allow about 25 requests a day, so non-US prices update when you select a stock or tap Refresh. Non-US exchange suffixes: .LON London, .TRT Toronto, .TRV TSX Venture, .DEX Germany (XETRA), .BSE Mumbai, .SHH Shanghai, .SHZ Shenzhen, .KS Korea (e.g. 005930.KS for Samsung Electronics). Korea support on Alpha Vantage is unconfirmed; if no price loads, type one manually below. Quotes may be delayed.
